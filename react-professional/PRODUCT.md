# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Farmers looking to rent agricultural machinery, and equipment owners and operators who provide it.

## Product Purpose

Kissan Markaz connects farmers with equipment owners and operators so they can rent agricultural machinery. The home page should help visitors discover equipment and find the account path that fits them.

## Positioning

A marketplace centered on connecting farmers with local machinery owners and operators.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Account registration supports Farmer, Owner, and Operator roles. Do not imply equipment availability, prices, locations, or completed bookings without real product data.

## Brand Commitments

Keep the Kissan Markaz name and the existing green agricultural feel. No logo has been supplied.

## Evidence on Hand

The existing signup page and its registration form are in `src/pages/signup.jsx`. No verified equipment listings or brand imagery are supplied.

## Product Principles

- Make it easy for farmers to find the right kind of machinery.
- Keep the paths for farmers, owners, and operators clear.
- Do not present illustrative equipment information as live inventory.
